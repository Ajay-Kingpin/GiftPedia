## 🧪 Demonstration Workflow

### **🎯 Step 1: Occasion Selection**
**User Action:** Select gift occasion from visual cards
**Options:** Birthday, Anniversary, Wedding, Graduation, Holiday, Just Because
**AI Response:** Context-aware recommendation engine activation

### **🎯 Step 2: Recipient Identification**
**User Action:** Choose recipient type
**Options:** Mother, Father, Spouse, Friend, Child, Colleague
**AI Response:** Personalization parameters adjustment

### **🎯 Step 3: Detailed Preferences**
**User Action:** Provide age, interests, budget, additional details
**Fields:** Age range, interests/hobbies, special requirements, budget (INR)
**AI Response:** Complex preference analysis and matching

### **🎯 Step 4: AI Recommendations**
**System Response:** Personalized product recommendations
**Features:** Product cards with images, pricing, availability, affiliate links
**User Action:** View details, shop via affiliate links
